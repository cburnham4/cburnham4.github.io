Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · IOU Tracker · CVHB LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how CVHB LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information in the IOU Tracker mobile app (Android package / iOS bundle com.cvhb.loantracker). CVHB LLC is located at 3915 Beryl Road, Suite 130, Raleigh, NC 27607.

Overview

IOU Tracker helps you keep track of money you lend and borrow. The app works offline. Loan data is stored locally on your device. Sign-in is optional. If you sign in, your account details and loan records can sync across your devices. The app is free and shows ads. We do not sell personal data. There are no payments in the app.

Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • On-device loan data — people names, amounts, dates, notes, and reminders you enter are stored locally on your device so the app works offline. Uninstalling the app typically removes this local data.
  • Optional account information — if you choose to sign in via Firebase Authentication (email/password, Google, or Sign in with Apple), we store your email address and name associated with that account so we can identify you and sync your data.
  • Synced loan records — when you are signed in, your loan records (people names, amounts, dates, notes, and reminders) sync to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so you can use them across devices signed into the same account.
  • Local notifications — the app may show local notifications on your device for reminders you set. These notifications are delivered on your device and are not used to send you marketing messages from us.
  • Advertising — the app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers / advertising ID and usage data for advertising and measurement. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not process payments in the app
  • Sign-in is optional; you can use the app with data stored only on your device

Third-Party Services

IOU Tracker uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — optional sign-in (email/password, Google, Sign in with Apple) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore — sync of loan records for signed-in users — Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob — advertising — Google Privacy Policy

Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Synced account and loan data for signed-in users is retained while your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us through our Support page. We will process deletion requests for account and synced data.

Children’s Privacy

IOU Tracker is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Use the app without signing in; data stays on your device
  • Sign in only if you want sync across devices
  • Control notification and tracking permissions in your device settings
  • Limit ad tracking / advertising options where available on your device
  • Request account and data deletion via Support
  • Uninstall the app to remove most on-device app data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the current version. Check this page for the latest version.
