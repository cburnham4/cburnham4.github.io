Delete your Lift Tracker account and data

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · CVHB LLC

This page explains how to request deletion of your Lift Tracker account and data. Lift Tracker is published by CVHB LLC (formerly Let’s Hang LLC). It includes the Android app Lift Tracker (package letshangllc.allfitnesslift) and the Weight Lift Tracker iOS app, which share the same account.

How to request deletion

Email cburnhamiv@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Lift Tracker account.” We’ll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (email address and user ID) and all workout data synced to our servers (exercises, routines, sets, reps, weights).

What may be kept

Nothing tied to your account is retained. Anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and advertising data held by Google AdMob are governed by Google’s retention policies and aren’t linked to your Lift Tracker account.

Partial deletion

You can also ask us to delete only your workout data and keep the account, using the same email.

Privacy policy

See our Privacy Policy for more information about how we handle data.
