Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Sunrise & Sunset Times by CVHB LLC

CVHB LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) publishes Sunrise & Sunset Times (formerly under Let’s Hang LLC). This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Overview

The app shows sunrise, sunset, and twilight times, saved places, and local weather. We do not require an account and we do not sell your personal information.

Location

If you allow location access, the app uses your current location on your device to calculate sun times and request local weather for that place. Location is used to provide the feature you asked for; we do not use it to build a profile of you.

You can deny location access and still use the app with manually saved places.

Saved Places

Places you save (city names and coordinates) are stored locally on your device so you can switch between them. Deleting the app removes this data.

Weather Data

Weather forecasts are requested from a weather provider (OpenWeather) using the location or place you selected. That provider’s terms and privacy policy apply to the weather request.

Advertising

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers according to its policies: policies.google.com/privacy

You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising / Tracking), depending on your iOS version.

Analytics

The app may use Firebase Analytics to understand basic usage (for example, that the app was opened) so we can improve reliability and features. Analytics data is handled under Google’s Firebase / Google privacy terms.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contacts in the app
  • We do not sell personal information

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Check this page for the latest version.

Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Visit our Support page for Sunrise & Sunset Times.

By using Sunrise & Sunset Times, you agree to this privacy policy.
