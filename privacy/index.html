Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · CVHB LLC (successor to Let’s Hang LLC apps)

CVHB LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) publishes mobile apps, including apps previously associated with Let’s Hang LLC. This policy explains what data our apps use and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

Unless an app’s listing says otherwise, we do not collect, store, or share personal information on our own servers. Some apps send limited content you choose to submit (for example, text or a URL to verify) to a third-party AI provider for processing. That content is not stored by us as a personal profile.

Truth Finder AI

  • Text and URLs you verify are sent to OpenAI for analysis
  • No personal information is collected or stored by us
  • No analytics or tracking by CVHB LLC
  • Verification history and favorites are stored locally on your device
  • Local data never leaves your device except for the verification request sent to OpenAI
  • Delete the app to remove all local data

Other Apps

Many of our apps (fitness, weather, utilities, and similar) keep data on your device and do not require an account. If an app uses additional services, those are disclosed in the App Store privacy labels for that app.

Third-Party Services

Some apps use OpenAI’s API. Their privacy policy applies to data processed through their service: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

Some apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google’s policies apply: policies.google.com/privacy

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Check this page for the latest version.

Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Visit our Support page.

By using our apps, you agree to this privacy policy.
