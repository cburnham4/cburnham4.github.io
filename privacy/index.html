Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · CVHB LLC

CVHB LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) publishes mobile apps, including apps previously associated with Let’s Hang LLC. This policy describes how our apps handle information. Each app’s App Store privacy labels list the data types that apply to that specific app.

Overview

We build simple utility, weather, fitness, and AI-assisted apps. Most do not require an account. We do not sell personal information. Practices vary by app; only the features you use apply to you.

Information We May Use

  • On-device data — settings, saved places, history, favorites, or workout-related entries you create may be stored locally on your device. Deleting the app typically removes this data.
  • Location — some apps (for example weather or sun-time apps) may use location when you allow it, to show times or conditions for your area or a place you choose. You can deny location and often still use the app with manual places.
  • Content you submit — some apps may send text, URLs, or similar content you choose to enter to a third-party service (such as an AI provider) solely to provide the feature you requested.
  • Technical / usage data — some apps may use analytics (for example Firebase Analytics) for basic usage events so we can improve reliability and features.
  • Advertising — some apps show ads through Google AdMob. Google may use device or advertising identifiers under its policies.

Information We Do Not Collect by Default

  • We do not require an account for most apps
  • We do not ask for your name, email, or contacts inside most apps
  • We do not sell personal information

Third-Party Services

Depending on the app, we may use third-party services such as:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase / Google Analytics — Firebase Privacy
  • Weather providers (for example OpenWeather) when an app shows forecasts
  • AI providers (for example OpenAI) when an app analyzes text or URLs you submit — OpenAI Privacy Policy

Those providers process data under their own terms. See each app’s App Store privacy labels for what that app uses.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Your Choices

  • Grant or revoke location and tracking permissions in iOS Settings
  • Limit Ad Tracking / Apple Advertising options where available
  • Delete an app to remove most on-device app data

Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Check this page for the latest version.

Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Visit our Support page.

By using our apps, you agree to this privacy policy.
