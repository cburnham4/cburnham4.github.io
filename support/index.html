Help for apps published by CVHB LLC (including apps formerly under Let’s Hang LLC). We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

How to get help

  1. Open the app’s page on the App Store
  2. Use App Support or Report a Problem
  3. Include the app name, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue

You can also leave an App Store review with details — we watch those for bugs and feedback.
