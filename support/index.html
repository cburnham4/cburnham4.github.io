Support

Help for Sunrise & Sunset Times by CVHB LLC (formerly Let’s Hang LLC). We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

How to get help

  1. Open the Sunrise & Sunset Times page on the App Store
  2. Use App Support or Report a Problem
  3. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue

You can also leave an App Store review with details — we watch those for bugs and feedback.
